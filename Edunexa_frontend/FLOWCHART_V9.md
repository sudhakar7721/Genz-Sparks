# EduNexa V9 Login Flow

EduNexa
├── Student
├── Faculty
│   └── click Faculty → choose Faculty Login or HOD Login
│       ├── Faculty Dashboard + existing Faculty modules
│       └── HOD Dashboard + existing HOD modules
└── Management
    └── existing Management modules

The top-level card is labeled only `Faculty`. The HOD/Faculty choices are hidden until the Faculty card is clicked.
